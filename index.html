Personality Shapes Gender Bias in Persona-Conditioned LLM Narratives Across English and Hindi

An empirical investigation across 23,400 generated stories from six state-of-the-art LLMs, demonstrating that HEXACO and Dark Triad personality traits act as systematic amplifiers and attenuators of gender stereotypes in occupational contexts.

Overview

Why Persona Conditioning Alters Gender Stereotyping

While persona prompting enhances engagement and perceived realism in LLM applications, it fundamentally modulates how gender and professional competence are expressed in generated text.

Personality as a Bias Modulator

Personality traits act as systematic amplifiers and attenuators. Dark Triad traits consistently amplify male-stereotypic framing, while traits like Openness attenuate it.

Traits Often Trump Gender Labels

In multiple models, the regression effect of assigning a Dark Triad trait (e.g. Machiavellianism $\beta = +0.021$) exceeds the effect of explicit gender cues ($\beta = +0.011$ for male).

Bilingual English & Hindi Evaluation

Hindi exhibits higher baseline male skew due to cultural defaults and grammatical verb inflections, but English exhibits wider personality-driven variance.

23,400 Artifacts & 50 Occupations

Rigorous ecological validity: 50 Indian occupational scenarios (25 male-typed, 25 female-typed) evaluated across 6 diverse model architectures.

Step 1: Persona Attributes Specification
Personas are constructed systematically by combining occupational scenarios grounded in India with psychological trait descriptions from HEXACO and the Dark Triad.

How the Centroid-Based Metric Works

We represent gender stereotypes in a shared multilingual embedding space using male and female reference centroids derived from curated parallel word lists in English and Hindi (~200 items each covering nouns, verbs, and adjectives).

bias(si) = cos(si, cmale) − cos(si, cfemale)
Positive values indicate alignment with male stereotypes; negative values indicate alignment with female stereotypes.

Story-level aggregation uses peak salience: story(S) = bias(sk) where k = argmax |bias(si)|, prioritizing the most psychologically salient statement in the artifact.

Finding 1: Amplification vs Attenuation
Dark Triad Traits Amplify Male Bias

Machiavellianism ($\beta = +0.021$) and Psychopathy ($\beta = +0.018$) consistently amplify male-stereotypic framing across all model families, while Openness ($\beta = -0.006$) and Emotionality ($\beta = -0.004$) attenuate it.

Finding 2: Trait vs Gender Relative Influence
Personality Often Trumps Gender Labels

In multiple regression specifications, personality coefficients exceed the coefficient of explicit gender assignment ($\beta = +0.011$ for male), demonstrating that personality conditioning fundamentally alters behavioral expression.

Finding 3: Cross-Linguistic Differences
Hindi Morphological Saturation

Hindi exhibits a higher default male baseline skew due to grammatical verb agreement (करती हूँ vs. करता हूँ) and cultural defaults, while English demonstrates larger personality-induced swings.

Limitations

Important boundary conditions and caveats regarding the scope and methodology of this study.

Cultural Grounding of Personality Frameworks
HEXACO and Dark Triad frameworks originate from Western psychology and may not capture the full nuances of personality constructs across diverse Indian cultural contexts.
Centroid Lexicons & Translation Artifacts
Stereotype word lists were compiled in English and translated into Hindi with native speaker refinement, which may overlook region-specific colloquial expressions without direct English equivalents.
Single-Sentence Peak Salience Aggregation
Max-abs captures the most extreme stereotyping statement per narrative. While validated against mean and top-3 aggregation (87.2% agreement), alternative aggregations may highlight different distributional nuances.
Controlled Prompts vs. Multi-Turn Deployment
The study evaluates single-turn generation with structured prompts. Real-world chatbot conversations are interactive and dynamic, where personas may drift or intensify over extended dialogues.
